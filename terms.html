About CodyPrivacy policyPersonal-use terms

Cody personal is the owner's private local dashboard. These pages describe that application; they do not offer public dashboard accounts, subscriptions or a hosted data service.

Google connection

Access is voluntary and requires the Google account owner's explicit consent. The owner must be authorized to use the account and selected records. The implemented connection is read-only; selected snapshots can be partial and retain their actual read times. A failed read preserves the last successful snapshot and its date.

Operation

The application depends on the owner's Mac, local server, internet access, and valid provider authorization. This version does not promise continuous availability or automatic Google refresh schedules. It does not execute payments, trades or external messages.

Privacy and stopping access

Data handling and removal controls are described in the privacy policy. The owner can revoke Google access at any time in their Google Account settings and stop the local application.
