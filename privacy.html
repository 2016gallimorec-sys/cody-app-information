About CodyPrivacy policyPersonal-use terms

This policy covers Cody personal's read-only Google connection, optional bank-connection preparation and these app-information pages. Cody is operated by its owner for private use on their Mac.

What Cody accesses

Google consent permits read-only Gmail access and reading events on calendars the owner owns. Cody reads the authenticated mailbox address, the chosen message query, message identifiers, senders, subjects, timestamps, plain-text content or snippets, and read/unread labels. Cody does not request separate attachment downloads or save attachment files. Gmail’s full-message response can include inline MIME content; named attachment parts are excluded from the text Cody extracts for previews. It reads selected calendar events, including titles, times, locations, descriptions and recurrence details. The scopes are gmail.readonly and calendar.events.owned.readonly.

Purpose and limits

This information is used to display dated source records in the owner's local My Day workspace. Read controls use selected limits and date ranges, and disclose partial coverage. Cody does not send email, modify mailbox records or edit events. The connection does not run an automatic background sync schedule.

Storage and retention

Desktop client credentials and Google access/refresh tokens are stored in dedicated macOS Keychain entries. The browser does not persist them in local storage. Selected source snapshots, connection health and timestamps are saved in owner-accessible private files on the Mac and retained until refreshed or removed by the owner. Owner-created email-linked tasks/events, reply drafts and attention notes are stored separately in My Day and remain until individually removed. Local exported backups can retain earlier snapshots; they are plaintext unless the owner encrypts them. These records and credentials are excluded from the source-code repository. Local file permissions are not a claim of encryption or protection from other software running as the same Mac user.

Sharing and processing

Cody contacts Google's APIs for authorized reads and token renewal. The Google adapter does not send retrieved messages or events to an AI model, advertising service, data broker or analytics service. It does not sell Google data or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Separate discussion or sharing initiated by the owner is outside this adapter. An owner-chosen export or backup destination may receive the data the owner explicitly places there.

Your controls

The owner can stop reads and revoke Cody's authorization in their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revocation prevents further authorized reads but does not erase previously retained local snapshots or backups. To remove those, ask the Cody coordinator to review and remove the Google snapshots, connection metadata, any separately retained My Day tasks/events, drafts and notes, and dedicated Keychain entries locally, including reviewing retained backups. There is no self-service deletion control advertised by this version.

Optional bank account-information connection

Cody's current Enable Banking preparation reads only registered-application and supported-bank metadata. When explicitly approved, Cody stores the application ID and private signing key in a dedicated macOS Keychain entry; short-lived signed authentication tokens are sent only to Enable Banking. Disposable eligibility status and check times stay in private local files. This step neither connects a bank account nor reads bank records.

If the owner separately approves supported personal account linking and account-information access, Enable Banking and the bank process that authentication and consent. Bank sign-in takes place with the bank; Cody must not collect bank passwords. A future approved account-information connection would request only balances, account identifiers/currency and transactions needed for the local financial dashboard, retaining source dates and coverage. Any such implementation and actual read must be verified before being labelled connected. Payment initiation, trading and withdrawals are excluded. No bank schedule is enabled by this preparation.

Bank application credentials are excluded from Git, browser storage and private exports. Any later approved bank snapshots would be stored in private local files under the same retention/export boundary as other source snapshots. No bank data is sent by the adapter to AI, advertising or analytics services. The owner can stop the local server and revoke bank consent with the bank/provider; previously retained local records/backups need separate review and removal. Enable Banking's processing is described in its privacy notice. Account registration and bank grants are separate user decisions.

These information pages

These static pages contain no forms, tracking scripts, embedded dashboard records or Google authentication controls. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security under its privacy statement. That hosting does not receive Cody's Google tokens, mailbox content or calendar snapshots.

Google Limited Use

Cody personal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
